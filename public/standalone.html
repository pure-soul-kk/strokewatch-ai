THE F.A.S.T. EMERGENCY METHOD

If you observe ANY one of these symptoms, call emergency services immediately.

F
FACE DROOPING

Ask the person to smile. Does one side of the face droop down or look uneven?

A
ARM WEAKNESS

Ask the person to raise both arms. Does one arm drift downward or feel clumsy?

S
SPEECH DIFFICULTY

Ask them to repeat a simple sentence. Are the words slurred, garbled, or strange?

T
TIME TO CALL 911 / 112

Every second counts. 1.9 million neurons die every minute during ischemic stroke.
